# v0.11 draft release notes: What's New card for 0.11

Final lines for the v0.11 What's New card, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). Shipped as
`packages/core/main/default/customMetadata/Release_Note.V0_11_Whats_New.md-meta.xml`
(`Version__c` 0.11). The card reads the records with SOQL, so the detail lines are not cut at
255 characters; the headline is a 255-character Text field and stays well inside it.

Headline: **A simpler way to work with your data, plus faster saves**

- **Data Manager.** The one place for data tasks: Find and Export Records, Import a File,
  Update Many Records. Delete Many Records is last and asks you to confirm.
- **Import.** A guided wizard with a step path, cards for what you are importing, a trial run
  and a results file. Find and Bulk Update have a cleaner layout and menus.
- **Org Optimizer.** The one name for the data quality tool, with a Data Quality section.
- **Duplicate Matching.** Pairs side by side with Merge and Not a Duplicate; matching rules
  have an Active switch and Show Details.
- **BarnCRM Settings.** Grouped with icons and help popovers; a save bar stays in view; every
  setting shows its default.
- **Setup Checklist and Home.** Progress rings, one step count, status tiles with badges and
  quick links.
- **Record panels.** Member tiles, address cards and clear empty states.
- **Faster saves.** Address changes, household updates, rollups and gift allocations do less
  work on every save.
- **Polish.** Every tab has its own icon, labels are shorter, new-tab links say so, and Error
  Log is always the last tab.

The 0.6 card record stays: someone who never dismissed the card sees only the current release,
and someone who dismissed 0.6 sees 0.11 only.
