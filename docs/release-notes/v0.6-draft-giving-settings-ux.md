# v0.6 draft release notes: Receipt letters and giving settings

Draft lines for the v0.6 release notes, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). From the UI/UX
review, items 5 and 9.

- **Receipt letters are edited like an email.** The letter on the Receipts page is now a rich
  text editor with bold, italic, lists, headings and links, instead of HTML in a plain box.
  The tokens (`{{DonorName}}`, `{{Amount}}` and the rest) work as before.
- **The letterhead prints itself.** Your organization's name, address and logo print above
  every receipt and statement from your settings, so they are no longer part of the letter you
  edit. A letter you saved before keeps printing exactly as it did until you next save it.
- **Preview before you issue.** **Preview** shows the letter filled in for a sample gift, with
  the tax sentences and signature BarnCRM adds. Nothing is issued and no number is used.
- **View PDF.** The Receipts panel on a gift has a **View PDF** link beside each receipt.
- **The receipt screens can be translated.** Every heading, button and message on the
  Receipts panel and the Receipts page is now a custom label.
- **Thank you rules: pick the email by name.** **New rule** and **Edit** on the
  Acknowledgments page open the rule form on the page, with the email template chosen from a
  list of your templates by name. No more copying developer names.
- **Stewardship plans: no plan key to invent.** A new plan's key is made from its name and
  never shown, and renaming a plan keeps it.
