# v0.6 draft release notes: moving a pilot to the managed packages

Draft lines for the release notes, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). Feature C-31, planned
for v0.11 and built early; it ships no package component, only a kit in the repository and its
guide page.

- **Pilot organizations have a way onto the listed packages.** Salesforce cannot switch an
  installed early edition of BarnCRM to the edition on AppExchange. The new page
  [Moving a pilot to the managed packages](../admin-guide/moving-to-managed-packages.md) walks
  through copying your BarnCRM records off, removing the early packages, installing the listed
  ones and loading your records back, with a kit that does the copying, the loading and a
  record-by-record check.
- **People, households and organizations are never removed.** Only what BarnCRM added to them,
  and its own records, are copied and put back.
- **Rehearse first.** The page asks for a full run on a sandbox copy of your org before the real
  one, and the kit's check step must report no differences there.
- **What does not come across:** field history, the undo window for past imports, and running
  record numbers, which are reissued in their original order. Receipt numbers are kept exactly.
- **Not yet confirmed on a real org.** The kit is tested without an org; the first rehearsal on
  a pilot's sandbox confirms the points listed in the contributor guide.
