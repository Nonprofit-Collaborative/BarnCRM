# What's New

## What It Does

After BarnCRM is upgraded, everyone who opens the BarnCRM home page sees a short **What's new
in BarnCRM** card at the top: a headline and a few lines for each change worth knowing about.
Each person sees it once for each release. When they click **Dismiss**, it goes away for them
until the next upgrade brings something new.

Nobody has to read release notes on a website or remember to tell the team what changed: the
notes come with the upgrade itself.

## How to Turn It On

There is nothing to turn on. The card is part of the BarnCRM home page as soon as BarnCRM is
installed, and it fills itself from the notes each release ships.

- **Who sees it:** everyone with a BarnCRM role (Admin, Team Member, Coordinator or
  Read Only) who opens the BarnCRM home page.
- **What they see:** the notes for every release since they last dismissed the card, newest
  first. Someone who has never dismissed it sees only the notes for the current release, not
  the whole history.
- **When it disappears:** when that person clicks Dismiss. Dismissing is personal: it hides the
  card for the person who clicked, not for anyone else.
- **Modules:** a module such as Giving can ship its own notes. They appear in the same card,
  under the same release, marked with the module's name. Only installed modules' notes
  appear, so a Standard Edition org without nonprofit modules sees Core's notes alone. The
  edition is chosen once in the Setup Assistant; [Editions](editions.md) explains it.

## A Five-Minute Walkthrough

1. Open the **BarnCRM** app and the **Home** tab. Result: at the top of the page, a card
   headed "What's New in BarnCRM" lists the notes for the current release under "Version" and
   the release number.
2. Read the first note. Result: a bold headline, and under it a line for each detail.
3. Click **Dismiss**. Result: the card disappears.
4. Refresh the page. Result: the card stays gone. It will come back only when an upgrade adds
   notes for a newer release.
5. Ask a colleague with a BarnCRM role to open the home page. Result: they still see the card,
   because each person dismisses it for themselves.

## Common Mistakes

- **"I dismissed it by accident and want to read it again."** A dismissed card does not come
  back until the next release. The same notes are in the release notes published with each
  version.
- **"A colleague says they never saw the card."** They see it only on the BarnCRM home page,
  and only with a BarnCRM role. If they use a different home page, or hold no BarnCRM role,
  assign them a role from BarnCRM Settings, Access.
- **"The card shows notes from more than one release."** That person had not opened the
  home page since an earlier upgrade, so they see everything they missed, newest first.
  Dismiss once to clear all of it.
- **"The notes are in English in our translated org."** The notes are written in English and
  are not translated in this version.
