# Donor Scores

Every donor with a gift has a score for how recently they gave, how often and how much, and
a giving pattern that sums them up. You see them on the donor's record and can list donors by them.
Nobody types them in: they update by themselves as gifts arrive and as time passes.

**Before you start:** you need a Giving permission set. Your admin sets the cutoffs (see
[Donor scoring](../admin-guide/donor-scoring.md)).

## To See a Donor's Score

1. Open the person, household or organization. The **Details** tab is open.
2. In the **Giving** section, read **Giving Pattern**. In **Giving Details** below it, read
   **Donor Score**.
3. **Recency Score**, **Frequency Score** and **Amount Score** each run from 1 to 5, where
   5 is best. **Days Since Last Gift**, **Recent Gift Count** and **Recent Giving** are the
   numbers behind them (the last two years of giving unless your admin changed it).

A donor with no gifts has no score.

## What the Giving Patterns Mean

- **Champion:** gave recently, often and generously. Thank them personally.
- **Loyal:** gives regularly and recently.
- **Promising:** gave recently but only once or twice. A good time for a second ask.
- **At Risk:** used to give often or generously, but not lately. Worth a personal note.
- **Lapsed:** has not given in a long time.
- **Occasional:** gives now and then.

## To See a Person's Wealth Screening

If your organization has loaded a wealth screening, a person's **Giving Details** section also
shows **Capacity Rating**, **Affinity Rating**, **Screening Score**, **Screening Date** and
**Screening Source**, as the screening vendor gave them. They are separate from the donor
score, which uses giving only. You see them with the Giving Admin or Giving Staff permission
set, not with Giving Read Only (see [Wealth screening data](../admin-guide/wealth-screening.md)).

## To Make a List of Donors by Score

- In **Donor Segments**, choose the **Champions** or **Slipping Donors** template and save it.
  See [Donor segments](donor-segments.md).
- In **Find**, choose **Contacts** or **Accounts**, add **Giving Pattern** or **Donor Score** as a
  column or a condition, and save the search.
