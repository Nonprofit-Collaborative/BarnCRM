# Recurring Donor Health

Recurring Donor Health shows what your monthly donors bring in and which of them may be about
to stop: a failed charge, a card about to expire, a cancellation at the payment processor, or a
payment that is overdue. Setup detail: [Recurring donor health](../admin-guide/recurring-donor-health.md).

## To See Your Recurring Donors

1. In the **BarnCRM Fundraising** app, on **Fundraising Home**, click **Recurring Donor
   Health** under **More**.
2. The top shows **Monthly Recurring Revenue**, **Active Recurring Gifts** and **Monthly
   Revenue at Risk**.
3. **Recurring Gifts Received, Last 12 Months** shows each month's total.

You see the recurring gifts you have access to, so your figures may differ from a colleague's.

## To Save a Sustainer

1. Under **Save the Sustainer**, each line shows the donor, the gift and why it is listed.
2. Choose **Create Follow-Up Task** to add a task for yourself, due today.
3. Open the gift to see its **Failed Charges**, **Last Failed Charge**, **Card Expires** and
   **Cancelled at Processor**.
4. If the donor has really stopped, set the commitment's **Status** to Cancelled. BarnCRM never
   does that for you.

## When Events Are Unmatched

If the page says some processor events match no recurring gift, open **Recurring Gift Events**,
choose the **Unmatched Events** list view, open each event and choose its **Commitment**. The gift
is updated as soon as you save.
