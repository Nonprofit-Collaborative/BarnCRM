# Ask Reminders

Applies to: BarnCRM main as of 2026-10-31 (G-29).

Ask reminders tell you when it is time to ask a donor again. You set a date on the donor. When it arrives, a task appears in your task list.

## To Set a Next Ask Date

1. Open the person, household or organization in the Fundraising app.
2. Click **Edit**, set **Next Ask Date** to the day you should ask again, and, if you just spoke with them, set **Last Touch Date** to today.
3. Click **Save**.

You should see: nothing yet. The reminder appears on the morning of the date. If the **Next Ask Date** field is not on your page, ask your admin to add it ([Ask cadence reminders](../admin-guide/ask-cadence.md)).

While the donor has an open ask with a **Target Date**, the earliest one sets **Next Ask Date** for you and replaces a date typed here.

## To Work a Reminder

1. Open your tasks. A task named "Ask (donor name): next ask is due" is due on the Next Ask Date. It is assigned to whoever owns the donor.
2. Make the ask.
3. Open the donor, set a new **Next Ask Date** and update **Last Touch Date**, then complete the task.

You should see: a new reminder on the new date. Changing the date to another date that has already arrived makes a new task straight away at the next 3:30 AM run. Completing or deleting the old task does not bring it back.

## To See Who Is Due

1. Open the **Contacts** or **Accounts** tab.
2. Choose the list view **Who Is Due for an Ask**.
3. Click the **Next Ask Date** column to put the longest overdue first.

You should see: every donor whose Next Ask Date is today or earlier, whoever owns them, including dates too old to get a task.
