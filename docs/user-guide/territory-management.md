# Territory Management

Territory Management puts people, households and organizations into territories and gives them
an owner, by rules your admin sets. Your admin turns it on; the setup is in the Admin Guide's
[Territory Management](../admin-guide/territory-management.md) page.

## Your Records' Territory

A person, household or organization in a territory shows it in its **Territory** field, where
your admin has added the field to the page. You can:

- filter a list view or a report by Territory, or use it as a condition in Find;
- change a record's Territory by hand, if you hold Territory Staff or Territory Admin. The next
  assignment run leaves a record alone while it is in the territory its rules give it, and moves
  it if a rule of a higher priority territory matches.

When a record moves into a territory, its owner may change too: to the territory's owner, or to
one of the territory's members in turn. If you are a member of a territory, new records of that
territory may be given to you.

## Who Changed My Records

An assignment run is recorded in Data Manager, **Data Jobs**, as a bulk update by the admin who
ran it. If a run moved a record by mistake, ask your admin: they can undo the whole run.
