# Corporate Hub

Corporate Hub keeps your organization's policies, filings and contracts in one place. Your admin
turns it on; the setup is in the Admin Guide's [Corporate Hub](../admin-guide/corporate-hub.md)
page.

## The Corporate Hub Page

Open the **Corporate Hub** tab. It shows:

- **Policies to Acknowledge**: the policies you have been asked to read, with their due dates.
  Open the policy to read its file, then press **I Have Read This**. It disappears from the list.
- **Compliance Due**: obligations that are overdue or due in the next 60 days, soonest first,
  with their owner.
- **Agreements to Act On**: agreements whose notice date (or end date) is in the next 90 days.

Admins also see **Add Missing Templates**, which loads any shipped template the org does not
have yet.

## Library Documents

Each Library Document is a policy, procedure, governing document, plan or form, with its file in
the **Files** list. Status tells you whether it is a Draft, Active or Retired, and Version which
version is current. The **Policy** panel shows how many people have acknowledged the current
version and who is still outstanding. With edit access you can **Mark Reviewed** (the next review
date moves on); admins can also **Publish New Version**, **Retire** and **Request
Acknowledgments**.

## Completing a Compliance Obligation

Open the obligation (from the Corporate Hub page or the **Compliance Obligations** tab), do the
filing, then on the **Compliance** panel press **Complete This Cycle**. Choose the date it was
done, the outcome and any notes (a confirmation number, say), then attach the proof with
**Upload Files**. The due date moves on to the next cycle, and the cycle is kept in the
**Compliance Cycles** list.

## Agreements

The **Agreements** tab lists your contracts, leases and subscriptions. Keep the signed copy in
the agreement's **Files**. If you own an agreement, you get a bell notification before its notice
date, so there is time to renew, renegotiate or give notice.

## The Board

The **Boards and Committees** tab holds your board of directors and its committees. On a board's
page the **Board** panel shows who holds a seat now, whose term ends soon (and who has reached the
term limit), the skills the board wants that no member has, the board's open action items and,
when your org uses BarnCRM Giving, the share of members who gave this fiscal year. Add a seat
with **New** on the Board Members list: the person, role and term dates.

## Board Meetings

Create a meeting from the **Board Meetings** tab, then on its page:

1. Press **Invite Board** to add everyone with a current seat.
2. Attach the packet to the meeting's Files, and paste a link to it in **Packet Link** so
   members can open it from the board portal.
3. During or after the meeting set each person's attendance; the panel says whether you have a
   quorum.
4. Type the minutes, then press **Submit Minutes for Approval**. Members approve them in the
   board portal; an admin marks them approved once the board has.
5. Add motions on the meeting. **Open Voting** lets members vote in the portal; **Close Voting**
   records whether it passed.

Open action items from earlier meetings are listed on each later meeting until someone marks
them Done. To find a time before scheduling, leave Start blank, fill in up to three Poll Times
and press **Send Poll**; then press **Choose This Time** on the time that suits most people.

## Reminders

When you own an obligation, agreement or document, a bell notification reminds you before it is
due (30 days by default). One reminder goes per due date. When you own a board, you are reminded
once before each member's term ends.
